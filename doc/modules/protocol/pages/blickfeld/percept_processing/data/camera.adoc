[#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera]
= Camera

What a camera contributes to the perception output: its video, live and as recorded into the 
history. 
 
The pictures are passed on as the camera encoded them and decoded by whoever shows them, so a 
device needs no decoder of its own to record what a camera saw.

[#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera_Video_Format]
=== Format

How a camera's video is encoded, which is what a decoder is configured with.

.Available fields in blickfeld.percept_processing.data.Camera.Video.Format
|===
| Field | Type | Default | Unit

.2+| *codec* | string| - | - 
3+| RFC 6381 codec string, e.g. `avc1.4d4016`, as WebCodecs' `VideoDecoderConfig.codec` takes it.

.2+| *binary* | bytes| - | - 
3+| Decoder configuration the codec defines, as WebCodecs' `VideoDecoderConfig.description` 
takes it. For H.264 this is the `AVCDecoderConfigurationRecord` (ISO/IEC 14496-15) 
carrying the SPS and PPS.

.2+| *width* | uint32| - | px 
3+| Width of the picture.

.2+| *height* | uint32| - | px 
3+| Height of the picture.

|===

[#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera_Video_Picture]
=== Picture

One encoded picture of a camera's video.

.Available fields in blickfeld.percept_processing.data.Camera.Video.Picture
|===
| Field | Type | Default | Unit

.2+| *timestamp* | uint64| - | ns 
3+| Unix timestamp at which the camera captured the picture, from its own clock where it 
reports one, and the time the device received it otherwise.

.2+| *keyframe* | bool| - | - 
3+| Whether the picture decodes on its own. A decoder has to start at one of these.

.2+| *binary* | bytes| - | - 
3+| The encoded picture. For H.264 these are its NAL units, each prefixed with its length 
as 4 byte big endian (`avc` format).

|===

[#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera_Video_Segment]
=== Segment

A run of a camera's video between one keyframe and the next. 
 
The segments of consecutive history frames continue each other, so a camera's video over 
a range of frames is its segments one after the other. The first segment of a capture 
window starts at a keyframe, which is where decoding the video of that window starts.

.Available fields in blickfeld.percept_processing.data.Camera.Video.Segment
|===
| Field | Type | Default | Unit

.2+| *serial_number* | string| - | - 
3+| `config.DataSource.Camera.serial_number` of the camera it was recorded by.

.2+| *object_uuid* | optional string| - | - 
3+| UUID of the DetectedObject the camera was tracking while it was recorded, if any.

.2+| *format* | xref:blickfeld/percept_processing/data/camera.adoc#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera_Video_Format[Format] | - | - 
3+| How the pictures are encoded.

.2+| *pictures* | repeated xref:blickfeld/percept_processing/data/camera.adoc#_blickfeld_percept_processing_data_Camera_Video_Picture[Picture] | - | - 
3+| The pictures in the order they were captured.

.2+| *position* | optional xref:blickfeld/percept_pipeline/config/data_source.adoc#_blickfeld_percept_pipeline_config_DataSource_Camera_Ptz_Position[percept_pipeline.config.DataSource.Camera.Ptz.Position] | - | - 
3+| Where the camera was pointing while it recorded them. 
 
A picture on its own does not say which way the camera was facing, so replaying one 
cannot show where in the scene it was looking. Recorded beside the pictures, the 
aim can be drawn with them - and followed as a replay runs, rather than being stuck 
wherever the camera happens to point now. Unset for a camera that cannot be aimed.

|===
