= Service: Camera

CAUTION: This API is not yet officially released nor maintained.

Service for what a camera sees, as the other services here serve the pipeline's other output. 
 
Finding a camera and aiming it are the `percept_pipeline` `Camera` service's.

[#Stream]
== Stream

A camera's live video, for as long as the caller keeps the stream open. 
 
The first message carries the format to configure a decoder with; the pictures follow, 
starting at a keyframe, so something can be shown at once. The format is sent again whenever 
it changes, and is followed by a keyframe. The encoded video is the camera's own, passed on 
without being decoded or encoded again; every viewer of a camera shares the one stream the 
device receives from it.

[#_blickfeld_percept_processing_services_CameraStreamRequest]
=== Request

A request to receive a stream of a camera's video.

.Available fields in blickfeld.percept_processing.services.CameraStreamRequest
|===
| Field | Type | Default | Unit

.2+| *camera* | xref:blickfeld/percept_pipeline/config/data_source.adoc#_blickfeld_percept_pipeline_config_DataSource_Camera[percept_pipeline.config.DataSource.Camera] | - | - 
3+| The camera to stream video from, described as the data source describes one. 
 
A camera that is configured is recognised by its `serial_number` and served from the stream 
the pipeline already receives from it; one that is not needs its connection filled in and is 
served from a stream opened for this call alone.
